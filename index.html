About me

I am a Marie Skłodowska-Curie postdoc (HORIZON-MSCA-2024-PF-01, project AlgHOF) at the Institut de Mathématiques de Jussieu-Paris Rive Gauche (IMJ-PRG) attached to the Centre National de la Recherche Scientifique (CNRS) in France under the supervision of Harald Helfgott. Previously I was a postdoctoral researcher at the Álfred Rényi Institute of Mathematics in Budapest under the supervision of Balázs Szegedy and before I was a PhD student at the Autonomous University of Madrid under the supervision of Pablo Candela. My research interests include additive combinatorics and its relations with ergodic theory, probability and dynamical systems. I am also interested in the theory of convergence and generalization of deep neural networks.
